Kaapi Brewing & Blend Guide

Everything you need to brew the perfect cup of South Indian filter coffee

90:10 vs. 77:23 Chicory Ratio: Which Should You Choose?

If you enjoy nuanced, aromatic coffee flavor with gentle sweetness, choose our 90:10 Dakshin Heritage or 100% Arabica Velvet Crest. If you love thick, bold, hotel-style decoction that stands up to rich frothed milk, go with our 77:23 Dakshin Gold blend.

How to Make Thick Filter Coffee Decoction at Home

Add 3–4 tablespoons of filter coffee powder to the upper chamber of a traditional South Indian brass or steel filter. Press gently with the umbrella plunger, pour boiling water on top, and cover for 15–20 minutes. Mix the rich first-press decoction with hot boiled milk and sugar, then froth using a dabarah and tumbler.

What is Barrel-Fermented Specialty Coffee?

Our Barrel Fermented Coffee micro-lots are aged and fermented in seasoned oak casks or with natural fruit and honey infusions (Wine, Whiskey, Rum, Orange, and Pineapple) prior to roasting—imparting deep aromatic complexity without artificial flavorings.

Coffee Care & Storage

Preserve maximum aroma and freshness

Seal Firmly

Always squeeze excess air out of resealable pouches before zipping shut.

Airtight Storage

Transfer beans or ground coffee to an opaque, airtight canister for extra protection.

Cool & Dark Place

Keep coffee away from direct sunlight, humidity, and heat sources like stoves.

Use Bag Clips

For standard pouches, roll the top down tightly and clip firmly before storing.
